# FAQ

## How much of my listening history can I see?

We show your **last 50 played tracks** from Spotify's API. Spotify does not provide access to older history. The Leaderboard uses Spotify's own top-items data, which covers longer time periods (4 weeks, 6 months, all time).

## Is my data stored?

We only store what's needed for authentication (your Spotify account link and session). We do **not** store your listening history. All track and artist data is fetched live from Spotify when you load a page.

## How often is the data updated?

Every time you load a page, we fetch fresh data from Spotify. There's no caching of your listening history.

## Can I revoke access?

Yes. Go to [Spotify Account Apps](https://www.spotify.com/account/apps/) and remove Spotify Stats. You can also sign out from the Settings page in the app.

## Why do I need to sign in with Spotify?

We need to authenticate with Spotify to read your listening data. We use OAuth — you sign in on Spotify's site, and we never see your password.

## Does this work with Spotify Free?

Yes. Spotify Stats works with both Free and Premium accounts. The data we use (recently played, top items) is available for all Spotify users.

## I see "unable to get user info" after signing in — why?

This error can occur when Spotify returns limited or no profile data during sign-in. **If your Spotify profile has strict privacy settings** (e.g., private profile, hidden listening activity, or restricted profile visibility), Spotify may not provide the basic profile info we need to complete your account setup.

**To fix it:** In Spotify, go to **Settings → Privacy and social** and ensure your profile allows sharing basic info (display name, etc.). You can tighten privacy again after signing in — we only need it once to create your account.
