# Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home base. It gives you a quick snapshot of your listening activity.

## Stats Cards

Three cards summarize your last 50 played tracks:

- **Unique Tracks** — How many different songs you've played
- **Unique Artists** — How many different artists appear in your recent plays
- **Listening Time** — Total duration of your last 50 tracks

## Recent Tracks

A list of your 10 most recently played tracks, including:

- Album art
- Track name and artist
- When you played it (e.g., "2 hours ago")

Click any track to open it in Spotify. Use **View all 50 tracks** to go to the full Recent History page.
