# Recent History

Recent History displays your last 50 played tracks in a clean, searchable list.

## What You See

For each track:

- Album art thumbnail
- Track name
- Artist and album
- When you played it (e.g., "3 hours ago")

## Search

Use the search box to filter the list by:

- Track name
- Artist name
- Album name

Search is instant and filters the 50 tracks client-side.

## Export to CSV

Click **Export CSV** to download your filtered (or full) history as a CSV file. Useful for:

- Keeping a record of what you've played
- Analyzing your listening in a spreadsheet
- Sharing with other tools

The CSV includes: Track, Artist, Album, and Played At timestamp.
