# Leaderboard

The Leaderboard shows your most-played tracks and artists based on Spotify's official listening data.

## Time Ranges

You can switch between three time periods:

| Range | What It Covers |
|-------|----------------|
| **Last 4 weeks** | Your listening from the past month |
| **Last 6 months** | Your listening from the past half year |
| **All time** | Your overall top items (based on several years of data) |

## Tabs

- **Top Tracks** — Ranked list of your most-played songs with album art, artist, and album name
- **Top Artists** — Ranked list of your most-played artists with profile images and genres

Click any item to open it in Spotify.
