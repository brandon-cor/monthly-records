# Getting Started

## Sign In with Spotify

1. Go to the [Spotify Stats app](/) and click **Sign in with Spotify**
2. You'll be redirected to Spotify to authorize the app
3. Grant the requested permissions (we only read your listening data — we never modify your library or playlists)
4. You'll be redirected back to your dashboard

## Permissions We Request

When you sign in, we request these Spotify scopes:

| Scope | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| `user-read-recently-played` | Fetch your last 50 played tracks |
| `user-top-read` | Fetch your top tracks and artists |
| `user-read-currently-playing` | Optional: show what you're playing now |
| `user-read-email` | Display your profile (when available) |
| `user-read-private` | Access your public profile info |

You can revoke access at any time from your [Spotify account settings](https://www.spotify.com/account/apps/).

## First Steps

After signing in:

1. **Dashboard** — See your overview and recent tracks
2. **Leaderboard** — Explore your top tracks and artists
3. **Recent History** — Browse and search your last 50 plays
4. **Settings** — View your account info or sign out

## Troubleshooting

**"unable to get user info"** — If you see this after authorizing with Spotify, your profile privacy settings may be blocking access to basic info we need. See the [FAQ](/faq#i-see-unable-to-get-user-info-after-signing-in--why) for how to adjust your Spotify privacy settings temporarily.
