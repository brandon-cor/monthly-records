# Introduction

Spotify Stats is a personal dashboard that surfaces your Spotify listening data in a clearer, more useful way.

## The Problem

Spotify's built-in listening history is limited. You can only see your last 50 played tracks in the app, and there's no easy way to browse your top tracks and artists across different time periods.

## The Solution

Spotify Stats connects to your Spotify account and gives you:

- **Recent History** — Your last 50 played tracks in a searchable list with album art, artist names, and timestamps
- **Leaderboard** — Your top tracks and artists by last 4 weeks, 6 months, or all time
- **Dashboard** — Quick stats: unique tracks, unique artists, and total listening time from your recent plays

All data is fetched live from Spotify's API. We don't store your listening history. We only use your Spotify login to read your data and display it.

## Who Is This For?

Anyone who wants a better view of their Spotify listening habits. Whether you're curious about your most-played songs, want to export your recent history, or just prefer a cleaner interface than Spotify's native options.
